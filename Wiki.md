## Intro

That One Dutch Guy is a Dutch <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTuber" target="_blank">YouTuber</a>, gamer, animator, memer and <a href="https://sm64community.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Mario_64_Machinima" target="_blank">Super Mario 64 machinimist</a> who started his <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube" target="_blank">YouTube</a> YouTube channel on June 30, 2014.

He announced his retirement from YouTube on the 31st of December 31st 2025 via a video named <a href="https://youtu.be/vPW2j9KUXtU?is=K2M1-py5SEAYrgjP" target="_blank">"Goodbye".</a>

## About

After creating his channel on June 30, 2014, he had not posted any videos until July 15, 2022, posting his first video which is called <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHUS9diWlz4" target="_blank">"Dumb things in Grand Theft Auto V"</a>, being a low quality raw unedited gameplay video of the 2013 game directly from his old <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_4" target="_blank">PlayStation 4.</a> He made these raw, unedited, unscripted recordings of his gameplay, mostly from Grand Theft Auto V, but sometimes he played other games too like Minecraft, Terraria, Cooking Simulator, and Planet Coaster. He spammed these videos out really quickly all time during this early era of the channel, until near the end of 2022. That's when he got his first gaming PC.
He then shifted to raw unedited recordings of various chaotic modded Grand Theft Auto V story-mode stuff, like flying a cargo plane loaded with pedestrians high into the air and opening the rear door while trying to perform a looping.
These were a bit more scripted than the old PS4 recordings, but still had a rather loose chaotic feeling to them.
He started doing small edits of his Grand Theft Auto V a while later, using ClipChamp in the early days. <br>

On April 6 2023, he made his first <a href="https://youtu.be/ug_OUInl_ag?si=QGexVt0fqKvtHJqr" target="_blank">2D animated video</a>, which was a short low quality video of a GIF of Rick Astley placed on a PNG cutout of a bicycle, roughly moving over a background image of Amsterdam. This marked the starting point of his series <a href="https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLlSKLjHQ3CPAeNTg3sRhdZ5FcqMzziei5&si=_hO0FAczEddojO54" target="_blank">Rick Astley's Adventures</a>, which increasingly got better in terms of animation. The series ended up getting 76 videos in total. He has stated that YouTuber <a href="https://youtube.com/@justahappytroll" target="_blank">Just A Happy Troll</a> with his funny Rick Astley videos were the main inspiration behind creating his own series.

On June 16, 2023, he decided to make his own "Super Mario 64 Bloopers" videos, starting with his own series, which is called <a href="https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLlSKLjHQ3CPB0Zor90Qv4Q3vbvvu0Itdy" target="_blank">"Mario and Bowser bloopers"</a>, which only lasted two short episodes and one Short. Afterwards, on August 2 of the same year, he made his first regular SM64 Bloopers video, which is called <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1u90kOQIiq4" target="_blank">"Super Mario 64 bloopers Episode 1: Mario's spaghetti."</a>
He ultimately made only three Mario and Bowser bloopers videos and four regular Super Mario 64 Bloopers episodes.

His main inspiration to make his SM64 Bloopers'' videos was the Australian SM64 machinimist <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@SMG4" target="_blank">SMG4</a>, being also a fan of the legacy version of this same machinimist from around 2013 to 2015.

He has also made videos of random memes, greenscreen meme videos, a library of frequently used meme soundeffects by legacy SMG4, Garry's Mod videos, YouTube Poop videos, and much more during his time active on YouTube.

He also made various Shorts during his time active. One of his major hits was a small brainrot series made in Garry's Mod regarding the Freddy Fazbear Trash Bin meme trend, which he later said he regrets making them because of a supposed "Gen Alpha vibe" hanging around those videos in his own words.

He also made a 5 episode series specifically for his Dutch subscribers where he did funny vulgar text to speech voiceovers of the Pat & Mat series (known as Buurman & Buurman in the Netherlands). These were uploaded randomly, with the first episode <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lg3hoCzE9rA" target="_blank">Buurman & Buurman Pat&Mat Nederlandse Voice Over Deel 1: Verdomde Picknick</a> uploaded on May 30, 2023. The first four episodes were all made in 2023. The final episode <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IyTrraIhkJE" target="_blank">Buurman & Buurman Pat&Mat Nederlandse Voice Over Deel 5: Tyfus Doos</a> was uploaded on Dec 4, 2024.

His last real video before Goodbye was uploaded was called <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p4OJEOwDysM" target="_blank">Explosion... but different</a>, which was just a stutter loop of the explosion meme video. It was uploaded on May 22, 2025.

## Retirement & Side Projects
That One Dutch Guy announced his retirement on the 31st of December 2025, by uploading a video named Goodbye and making a <a href="http://youtube.com/post/Ugkx7uhushY1tWwVH9pb23c1iWe_cJ06paO6?si=97Ze5M2G7c6Nfsvb" target="_blank">community post.</a> He said he was turning 18 years old soon and that his new responsibilities were leaving less time for YouTube. He also said that he was completely out of ideas for new videos for the channel and that he felt like it became more of a chore to maintain the channel. He simply wasn't enjoying making videos anymore.

After That One Dutch Guy retired and turned 18, he started rouding up his MBO IT study, which he finished. He seems to have a passion for making web-based games now. A few months after his retirement post, he <a href="http://youtube.com/post/UgkxMrpm7OGKsekgNmMW_9_0nRt-eRSLkZhV?si=cRVrHNx5xjvPoZTK" target="_blank">posted</a> a new community post, letting people know about his own new website. It's hosted on GitHub Pages and it's called <a href="https://that1dutchguy1.github.io/thatonedutchguys-shit-ass-toybox/">That One Dutch Guy's Shit Ass Toybox</a>. It used to be called That One Dutch Guy's Minigame Hub, before being moved to a new repository and rebranded to the Shit Ass Toybox. He maintains the <a href="https://github.com/That1DutchGuy1/thatonedutchguys-shit-ass-toybox" target="_blank">repository</a> still actively. It's still work in progress, but more web-games keep getting added and is in active development. The main site is completely stable, but some games are public Alpha's or Beta's. Most of the games on it currently as of writing this are finished, but sometimes still get fun updates. More than 10 games are currently already available to play on his website!

The games on the Shit Ass Toybox are mainly themed after classic memes and YouTube Poop from the 2000s and early 2010s.
